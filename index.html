📌 STUDY NOTES: ECOSYSTEMS & CODE

UNIT: 01 SUBJECT: Environmental Science TOPIC: Ecosystem Dynamics

🎯 Core Objective: Understand how living organisms interact with each other and their physical environment to form a functional, self-sustaining unit governed by energy flow and nutrient cycling.

1. Concept Breakdown & Key Terms

  • Ecosystem: Functional unit where living (biotic) components interact with physical (abiotic) environments.
  • Biotic Factors: Living entities including plants, animals, fungi, and bacteria.
  • Abiotic Factors: Non-living chemical/physical factors like sunlight, temperature, soil, and pH.

2. Computational Formula Code Snippet

••• 
# Formula: Net Primary Productivity (NPP) = GPP - Respiration
def calculate_npp(gross_primary_productivity, respiration_loss):
    """Calculates net energy available to herbivores."""
    net_productivity = gross_primary_productivity - respiration_loss
    return f"{net_productivity} kcal/m²/yr"

print(calculate_npp(10000, 4500))
